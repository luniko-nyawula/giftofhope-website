About us

About Gift of Hope Foundation

Gift of Hope Foundation is a South African non-profit organisation established in 2018 to support vulnerable children, unemployed youth, and disadvantage families.

Our Mission

Is to improve the lives of vulnerable communities by providing practical support, education and development opportunities.

Our Vision

To build stronger communities where everyone has access to opportunities that improve their quality of life.
