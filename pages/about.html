About Us

About Gift of Hope Foundation

Gift of Hope Foundation is a South African non-profit organisation established in 2018 to support vulnerable children, unemployed youth and disadvantaged families.

Our Mission

Our mission is to improve the lives of vulnerable communities by providing practical support, education and development opportunities.

Our Vision

Our vision is to build stronger communities where everyone has access to opportunities that improve their quality of life.
